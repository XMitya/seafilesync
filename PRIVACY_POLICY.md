# Privacy Policy for Seafile Sync

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

## 1. Overview
Seafile Sync ("the Application") is an open-source mobile application designed to sync files with user-designated Seafile servers. Your privacy is critically important to us.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
- **Personal Data:** The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) to the developer or any third-party analytics services.
- **Server Credentials & Data:** All account credentials (server URLs, usernames, passwords, or authentication tokens) and synchronized files are communicated exclusively between the Application and the user-specified Seafile server. This data is stored locally on your device in secure app storage.
- **Third-Party Services:** The Application does not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

## 3. App Permissions
The Application requests only the permissions strictly necessary to perform its core functionality:
- **Internet Access:** Required to connect to your specified Seafile server.
- **Storage Access / All Files Access:** Required to read, write, and synchronize files on your local device storage.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this repository.
